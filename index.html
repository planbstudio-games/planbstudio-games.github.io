Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Applies to all Plan B Studio games and this website

Plan B Studio ("we", "us") makes free, ad-supported mobile games: BombBoi, Ball Fusion, Free Throwaway, Rotate to Fit, Tilt Death, Pizza Dash, Word Play and Note Drop. This policy explains what information our games and website collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. Our games have no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Your game progress, scores, levels and settings (like sound on or off) are saved only on your device. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

  • Personalized ads: On Android, open Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. Once deleted, apps can't use it for personalized ads.
  • Consent: If you're in a region that requires consent, such as the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game will ask before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings.

Device permissions

Some games use your phone's motion sensors to control gameplay (for example, tilting the phone in Tilt Death). Sensor readings are used on your device only, in real time, and are not stored or sent anywhere. The games may use internet access so ads can load.

This website

This site has no sign-ups, forms or tracking. It remembers which game category you last filtered by, saved only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

Because we don't collect or store your personal information on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or lose. Data collected by Google for ads is kept under Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
