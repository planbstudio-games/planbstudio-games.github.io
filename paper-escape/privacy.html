Paper Escape
Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Plan B Studio

Paper Escape does not collect, store or sell personal information. The only data collected comes from Google's ad service, which keeps the game free.

Paper Escape (“the game”) is a mobile game made by Plan B Studio (“we”, “us”) for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

The game itself collects nothing about you

The game has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. Your progress (best distance, stars, unlocked paper styles) and your settings are saved only in a file on your device. We never receive them.

Ads are served by Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example, to keep running). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads and the game;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that show its ads at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You choose how ads use your data

  • In the EEA, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your answer at any time from Settings › Privacy choices in the game.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings › Google › Ads (the exact menu varies by device).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. For data collected by Google's ad service, use the choices above or Google's privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
