Privacy Policy

Ball Fusion · Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plan B Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Ball Fusion (the "App").

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

Stored on your device only: your game progress (such as level, score, and learning progress) and your settings (such as volume). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising

The App shows ads provided by third-party advertising services ([AD NETWORK NAME, e.g. Google AdMob]). These services may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, and information about how you interact with ads, in order to show ads and measure their performance. They may use this information to show you personalized ads, depending on your device settings and where you live.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more about how the advertising service handles data in its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en.

Analytics and crash reports

Google Play may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance information (such as device model and Android version) through Google Play Console. We do not use this to identify individual users.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to run the App, show ads, and keep the App working well. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except the advertising services described above, and where required by law.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App. We do not keep a copy of it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and the limited information it handles, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
