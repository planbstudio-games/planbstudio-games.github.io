Little Paws Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Little Paws does not ask for your name, email, or any account, and we (the developer) do not collect personal information about you. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to provide those ads, as described below.

This policy applies to the Little Paws mobile game for iOS and Android ("the App"), published by Plan B Studio ("we", "us").

Information the App stores

The App saves your game progress only on your device: levels unlocked, stars earned, lives and the life refill timer, sound and music settings, and which tutorial tips you have seen. This data is not sent to us, and we have no servers that receive it.

  • No account, sign-in, name, email, contacts, photos or precise location is requested.
  • There are no in-app purchases, so no payment information is handled.
  • Deleting the App deletes this saved progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional video ad for an extra life or extra moves, and an ad between levels after every few wins. When an ad is requested, the AdMob software in the App shares information with Google, including:

  • the App's name and your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS)
  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general location
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, and how you interact with ads

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if ad personalization is allowed, show more relevant ads. Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not receive your advertising ID or IP address; AdMob gives us only combined reports such as ad views and earnings.

Your choices

  • Consent message. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice any time with Privacy Settings on the title screen or in the pause menu.
  • Tracking on iPhone. iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and you still get ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Google ad settings. You can turn off ad personalization at Google My Ad Center.
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the App.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and you have concerns, contact us and we will help, including asking Google to stop personalized ads for that device.

Keeping, protecting and your rights

  • How long data is kept. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the App. Google keeps ad data according to its own privacy policy.
  • Security. Because we don't collect or store personal information on our side, there is no account data of yours for us to lose.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for ads. Since we hold no personal data about you, these requests are handled through the choices above and directly with Google. We do not sell personal information.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
