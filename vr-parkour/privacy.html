VR Parkour – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

VR Parkour ("the game") is made by Mega B Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

Information the game itself stores

Your progress (best times, medals, ghost runs, unlocked courses) and settings are saved only on your device. We do not have servers and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset progress" deletes it.

Two-phone races (local Wi-Fi)

When you race a friend, the two phones find each other and exchange game data (position, time, course choice) directly over your local Wi-Fi network. This data is not sent to us or to the internet and is not stored after the race.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads on its phone menus (never inside the headset) using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad interactions and app performance (e.g. crash and diagnostic data).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy & ad choices. Elsewhere you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast – to find the other phone for local races.
  • Vibration – for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Motion sensors – to track your head movement in the headset (no permission prompt; data stays on the device).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
