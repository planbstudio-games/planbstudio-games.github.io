Drone Surfers – Privacy Policy

Mega B Studio · Effective November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Drone Surfers game ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we don't collect or receive any personal information about you. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, music, vibration, steering), your progress (unlocked courses, best times, wins), your chosen racer and the player name you type in. This stays on your device. We never receive it.

Wi-Fi races with friends

When you host or join a Wi-Fi race, the game finds other phones on the same local Wi-Fi network and exchanges what's needed to play: your player name, your chosen racer, and your drone's position and race events during the race. This goes directly between the phones on that network. It is not sent to us or to any server on the internet. Anyone in the same race can see your player name, so don't use your real name if you'd rather not.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads (a banner in the menus, an occasional full-screen ad between races, and optional ads you can choose to watch to unlock a course). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crashes.

Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland) When you first open the game, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
Ad personalisation On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
Your saved data Delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Settings → Reset progress clears your progress.

Permissions the game uses

Internet & network stateTo load ads and to connect to other phones for Wi-Fi races.
Wi-Fi state & multicastTo find other players on the same Wi-Fi network.
VibrationFor crash feedback (you can turn it off in Settings).

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Security

We don't operate servers that store information about you. Information processed by Google is protected under Google's own policies and practices.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
