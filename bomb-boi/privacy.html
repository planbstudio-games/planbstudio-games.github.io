Bomb Boi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

About this policy

Bomb Boi doesn't ask for your name, email or an account, and Plan B Studio runs no servers that store your data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

This policy covers the Bomb Boi mobile game for Android and iOS, made by Plan B Studio ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We don't collect personal information ourselves. Bomb Boi has no sign-in, accounts or chat.

  • Saved progress and settings: unlocked levels, stars, best times and your sound and vibration settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Local Wi-Fi co-op: when you play with a friend, your two devices connect directly over your local Wi-Fi network. The host shows its local network address so the other player can join. This connection stays on your network and nothing from it is sent to us or stored.
  • Device features: the game uses network access (for co-op and ads) and vibration. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bomb Boi shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad break every few levels, and an optional ad you can watch for extra lives. Google decides how ad data is used and acts as an independent data controller.

According to Google's data disclosure for its Mobile Ads SDK, ads may collect and share with Google:

Data What it is Used for
IP address Your device's internet address, which can estimate your general location Ads, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers Android advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking Ads, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions App launches, taps, video views Ads, analytics
Diagnostics App launch time, performance and energy use Analytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit. Third parties, including Google, may use cookies, similar identifiers or IP addresses to serve and measure ads. See How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads load.
  • iPhone and iPad: choose "Ask App Not to Track", or turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and the advertising identifier isn't shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Google > Ads or Privacy > Ads.
  • Limiting ad tracking doesn't remove ads; they just become less personalized.

Children

Bomb Boi is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has shared personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or rent personal information. The only data that leaves the game goes to Google for advertising, as described above.

Keeping and deleting data

Your saved progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Google keeps ad data under its own rules; for AdMob, it partly anonymizes IP addresses after 9 months and anonymizes or deletes advertising ID data after 18 months.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it's used. We hold no personal data about you, so for ad data, use the choices above or contact Google. You can still contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
